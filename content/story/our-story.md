Write however you like. Prose is fine — headings, dates and structure are all
optional, and I will shape whatever turns up here into the stations.

The only thing that genuinely helps is a blank line between moments, so I can
tell where one ends and the next begins. Everything else I can infer.

If you want to point me at something specific, these markers are enough — but
they are hints, not a format:

  [photo]     this moment should hang as a photograph
  [letter]    this is a letter to her, not a memory
  [gift]      something that should make her smile
  [milestone] something that mattered
  [place]     somewhere real
  [song]      a song
  [when: november 2019]   roughly when it happened
  [photo: riga-bar.jpg]   the file in photos/

Don't tidy it. First-draft sentences with your own rhythm in them are worth
more here than polished ones.
